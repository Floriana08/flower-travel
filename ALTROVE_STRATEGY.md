# Altrove strategy

## Positioning

Altrove creates thoughtful journeys for travellers who want to experience a place with greater depth, curiosity and intention.

It is a boutique travel studio combining:

1. Personal travel experience
2. Independent editorial recommendations
3. Carefully designed itineraries
4. A developing personalised travel-planning service

Altrove is not a generic travel blog, a destination directory, or a full-service agency claiming to book the world. It is being built destination by destination.

## Target customer

Travellers who:

- Prefer fewer places, more carefully chosen
- Care about food, neighbourhoods, atmosphere and pacing
- Want independent recommendations rather than affiliate noise
- Are considering Europe first — especially Portugal, Italy and Spain
- May later want help designing a honeymoon, anniversary trip or longer route

They are not looking for “bucket list” packaging or empty luxury language.

## Site hierarchy

Primary:

1. **Home** — brand and offer
2. **Journeys** — curated itineraries (primary product surface)
3. **Destinations** — curated place hubs
4. **Journal** — editorial support (secondary)
5. **About** — Flor and the studio
6. **Plan a Trip** — enquiry / early access CTA

Supporting:

- Club / letters (`/club`)
- Contact, Privacy
- Existing article and route URLs preserved for SEO

## Current offer (real now)

- Editorial destination and journal content
- Route / itinerary notes already published (e.g. Portugal by train, Lisbon food walk, Amalfi coast tours)
- Club letter signup (mailto bridge until ESP is wired)
- Trip enquiry form for future personalised planning
- Honest “in development” labelling for unfinished journeys

## Future offer (not claimed as live)

- Signature itineraries sold or delivered as finished products
- Personalised itinerary design
- Honeymoon planning
- Hotel shortlists and experience recommendations
- Downloadable guides
- Travel consultations at scale
- Complete trip planning and booking

Copy on the site uses “coming soon”, “in development”, and “join the list” language where services are not operational.

## Content priorities

1. Deepen Portugal, Italy (Naples / Amalfi / Rome) and Spain hubs
2. Promote journeys over article volume on the homepage
3. Keep journal articles that answer real planning questions
4. Consolidate thin or overlapping pieces (see `CONTENT_AUDIT.md`)
5. Add cornerstone pages: How Altrove works, What to expect from planning, Honeymoons with Altrove

## Conversion funnel

1. Discover brand on homepage
2. Browse **Journeys** or a **Destination**
3. Optional: read a **Journal** note for trust
4. Join **Letters from Altrove** or submit **Plan a Trip**
5. Later: consultation / itinerary delivery once capacity exists

One primary action per major section; avoid competing CTA clusters.
